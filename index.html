Kharcha privacy policy

Kharcha is a personal expense ledger. It does not have accounts, and it does not send your ledger anywhere.

What stays on this phone

Expenses, notes, categories, budgets, and the optional daily reminder are stored in a database on the device. A backup file leaves the phone only when you export or share it yourself.

Unlock

If you turn on the lock, the phone’s fingerprint or face check runs on the device. Kharcha does not receive or store a biometric template.

Reminders

The reminder is scheduled locally. It is not a remote push, and it is not tied to an advertising identifier.

Deleting your data

Settings has a wipe control that deletes the database on this phone. Uninstalling the app removes it as well.
